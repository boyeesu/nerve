# Governance

Nerve currently uses a maintainer-led governance model appropriate for an
early-stage project.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who participates through issues, code, documentation, design, testing,
or community support.

### Reviewers

Recurring contributors trusted to review changes in specific areas. Reviewer
status is earned through sustained, constructive participation.

### Maintainers

Maintainers merge changes, manage releases, enforce community standards,
coordinate security response, and steward the project's technical direction.

The current lead maintainer is [Daniel Esuga](https://github.com/boyeesu).

## Decisions

- Routine changes are decided through pull-request review.
- Public contracts, architecture, governance, and project direction should
  begin as an issue or proposal.
- Maintainers seek rough consensus, but retain final responsibility when a
  decision is required.
- Security incidents and Code of Conduct reports may be handled privately.

## Becoming a reviewer or maintainer

Candidates are considered based on consistent contributions, sound technical
judgment, respectful collaboration, and demonstrated care for operator safety
and project users. Maintainers may invite qualified contributors as the project
grows.

## Changes to governance

Material governance changes require a public pull request and an explanation of
the motivation, expected impact, and transition plan.
